I've been knitting for 20 years and I've developed tools for knitters for a good week or two now. These tools came to exist because I needed them and couldn't find what I wanted.

## The tools

**Motif** came from trying to design colourwork patterns in image editors. I needed to draw a grid and see it tile, not navigate layers and filters.

## Also on this website
- **Updates**: Changelogs and announcements when new tools launch or existing ones improve
- **Blog**: When I make something that's better expressed through words than yarn or code.

## Find me elsewhere

You can find my knitting projects on [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/people/knitlings) and the source code for the tools is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/Knitlings).
